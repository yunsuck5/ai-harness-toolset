# Evidence and claim discipline

## Purpose and trigger

Read this rule before you:

- create or retain an evidence artifact or precision beyond an explicit request, active contract, or acceptance predicate;
- decide whether a rule, review cycle, required artifact, or recurring check whose utility has been challenged should remain; or
- present a concern as a named risk or limitation.

This is a judgment and authority rule, not an automatic enforcement mechanism. It does not create a scanner, registry, hook, sidecar, hidden state, evidence hash table, or new hard gate.

## 1. Added artifacts and precision need a current reason

Use the authorized goal and available inputs. Ordinary authorized read-only inspection, transient command output, outputs already required by the selected workflow, and acceptance evidence explicitly requested by the user or an active contract remain allowed.

Do not create or retain an additional persistent evidence artifact, report, per-file hash or preimage set, measurement, or heightened precision merely because it might be useful later. Admit the addition only when at least one of these is true:

1. the user explicitly requested it;
2. an applicable active contract or acceptance predicate requires it; or
3. a current consumer and the concrete failure the addition protects are identified.

Potential future usefulness by itself is not a current consumer. Keep the addition proportional to the identified use, and do not turn its existence into new authority for mutation, approval, or retention.

## 2. Prove or remove uses an owner gate

When observed cost, repeated zero benefit, or other evidence challenges the utility of an existing rule, review cycle, required artifact, or recurring check:

1. report the situation and the available evidence to the operator or responsible owner **before** registering a removal candidate or performing a deletion, demotion, or disablement;
2. treat retention as a positive claim: the retention claimant identifies the current consumer, the failure being protected, and the evidence that the mechanism provides that protection;
3. if that case cannot be made, propose removal or demotion as the default disposition; and
4. route the actual disposition through the owner's normal decision, approval, and revision boundary.

The mechanism's existence, history, or generic safety wording is not proof of current utility. This rule never authorizes immediate or automatic deletion, disablement, or policy rewriting. If the required owner decision is unavailable, stop after reporting the unresolved disposition.

## 3. Risk and limitation claims name what can fail

A named risk or limitation identifies both:

- the consumer, actor, system, or decision that is affected; and
- the function, outcome, or contract that could fail.

When either is unknown, report the uncertainty and the missing evidence as unknown. Do not promote a generic concern or unsupported possibility into a blocker, required artifact, extra precision, or reason to extend a review cycle.

Identifying a risk does not itself authorize mutation or override an applicable approval or safety boundary. If another active rule requires a concrete artifact or check, that requirement remains an active contract unless its owner changes it through the applicable revision path.
