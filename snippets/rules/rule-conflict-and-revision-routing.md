# Rule: Rule conflict and revision routing

Use this rule only when an identified active-rule owner and a concrete required operation or acceptance condition cannot both be satisfied by any compliant path. Cost, inconvenience, preference, age, or an ordinary interpretation question is not enough; if applicability is unclear, ask that owner.

- The applicable rule and any explicit compliant branch remain binding until an approved revision takes effect under the applicable owner's lifecycle.
- Finding a conflict, approving the surrounding task, or requesting revision grants no exception, waiver, force permission, or completion claim.
- Report the owner, blocked operation or acceptance condition, exact incompatibility, affected scope, and compliant alternatives checked. The report carries evidence and status only; it creates no priority, arbitration, hidden state, or completion claim.
- Ask the user to choose a compliant rescope, hold or drop the blocked work, or start the applicable owner's revision lifecycle. Starting revision changes no binding state and approves no mutation, commit, push, adoption, activation, or other owner gate.
